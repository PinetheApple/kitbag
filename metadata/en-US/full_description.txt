Kitbag is a practice toolkit for musicians: a metronome, a tuner, and a song
and stem player in one local-first Android app, with a realtime C++ audio core.

Metronome
- Set the tempo by swiping anywhere, tapping it in or typing it on a numpad.
- Italian tempo markings, from Largo to Presto.
- An editable row of beat lights: group and wrap beats, and set an accent on each.
- A polyrhythm row, subdivisions, tempo ramps, bar muting and a count-in.
- Import your own click sounds.
- Presets and setlists stored on the device, with a summary of each practice
  session and a practice history you can browse.
- Controls in the notification while the app is in the background.

Tuner
- Reads the microphone, with a choice of tunings, your own tunings and an
  adjustable A4 reference.

Songs and stems
- Import songs; Kitbag finds the tempo, beat grid and meter in the background.
- Play a song with a click locked to its bars, an A-B loop and a count-in.
- Slow a song down to half speed; each song keeps its own speed.
- Play a song from its separate stems, with volume, mute and solo for each.
- Change a stem song's speed and key.
- See song sections and follow synced lyrics.
- Save a stem mix as a WAV file.
- Import songs from StemDeck, as a file or from your own StemDeck server.
- Put stem songs in setlists and queue what plays next.
- Stem songs keep playing in the background, with controls in the notification.
- Single songs keep playing in the background too, with the same notification.
- A stem song played from a setlist or Up next has Next and Previous in the
  notification and on the lock screen.

Settings
- Calibrate the output latency per audio route, and back up or restore your
  library through files you choose.
- Keep your songs on an SD card, choose a light or dark theme, and keep the
  screen awake while you practise.

Not in this build: play-along with other apps.

Kitbag needs no account and contains no ads, analytics or trackers.
