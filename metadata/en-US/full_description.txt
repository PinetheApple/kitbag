Kitbag is a practice toolkit for musicians: a metronome, a tuner and a stem
player in one local-first Android app, with a realtime C++ audio core.

Metronome
- Set the tempo by swiping anywhere, tapping it in or typing it on a numpad.
- Italian tempo markings, from Largo to Presto.
- An editable row of beat lights: group and wrap beats, and set an accent on each.
- A polyrhythm row, subdivisions, tempo ramps, bar muting and a count-in.
- Presets and setlists stored on the device, with a summary of each practice session.
- Controls in the notification while the app is in the background.

Tuner
- Reads the microphone, with a choice of tunings, your own tunings and an
  adjustable A4 reference.

Songs and stems
- Import songs; Kitbag finds the tempo and beat grid in the background.
- Play a song from its separate stems, with volume, mute and solo for each.

Settings
- Calibrate the output latency per audio route, and back up or restore your
  library through files you choose.

Not in this build: play-along with other apps, and speed or key changes in the
stem player.

Kitbag needs no account and contains no ads, analytics or trackers.
