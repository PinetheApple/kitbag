Initial development build.

- Metronome performance surface: swipe-anywhere and tap-to-type tempo
  entry, beat LED row editor (grouping, wrapping, accents), polyrhythm
  row, bar sweep, preset steppers.
- Native realtime audio core (C++ / miniaudio) with phase-anchored
  playback and a BPM quarter-note denominator model (6/8 clicks six
  times per bar).
- Local preset store (on-device database; v7 schema).
