• Backups now keep stem songs in setlists. Restoring on a phone that lacks one of those stem songs says how many were left out.
• A database written by a newer version of Kitbag is no longer opened, so an older version can't change data it doesn't understand. Until the next release adds a screen for it, the library shows its existing "could not be opened" message in that case.
• You can move your library to an SD card and back from Settings → Data folder.
