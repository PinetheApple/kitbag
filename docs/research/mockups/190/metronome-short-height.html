Metronome at 360×480 dp (#190, item B) — layout options

Today the body below the app bar is one scroll view, but a vertical drag on the BPM block is the tempo swipe and wins. The same overflow hides the poly row under the pinned status card at 200% font while playing (item 8). The binding designs draw no short-height layout, so this needs a decision.
